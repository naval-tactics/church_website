🔑 Become Part of Our Family

To qualify for membership at South B Police Chapel, you are required to complete the following information during registration. Please provide accurate information. Your registration will be reviewed by church administration before membership is officially approved.

Matthew 16:19 - I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven

👤

1. Personal Information

Your basic personal details and identification information — Full name, ID/Passport, DOB, Gender, Marital status, Nationality. This helps us know you personally.

📞

2. Contact Information

Your phone, WhatsApp, email, and residential/contact details — Estate, City, Address so pastoral team can reach you.

⛪

3. Church Information

Your church attendance history, membership status, baptism information, and previous church details where applicable. How long attending South B?

✝️

4. Spiritual Information

Your Christian faith journey, salvation story, baptism interest, and areas where you may need spiritual support and prayer.

🎶

5. Ministry / Department

Your preferred church ministry, skills, talents, previous ministry experience, and area of service — Choir, Ushering, Youth, Media, Sunday School, etc.

🚨

6. Emergency / Family Contact

A person the church can contact when necessary — Name, relationship, phone, email. For pastoral care emergencies.

🔐

7. Account Information

Your member account credentials, acceptance of the church's terms and privacy policy, and communication preferences — Username, Email, Password. Secure and private.
